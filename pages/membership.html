Investment

Fees

Membership is billed per term, with a fee that varies by academy and level.

  • A term fee covers weekly classes, mentor time, shared tools and materials, and access to club events.
  • Competition travel (national or international) is billed separately and announced ahead of each event.
  • Exact fees depend on your academy and level — the team will confirm current pricing when you register.
  • A limited number of need-based scholarships are available every term.

Need a scholarship?

Mention it on the Join Now form below or in your registration, and our team will walk you through eligibility.

Before you go

Membership FAQs

A few quick answers about joining.

How quickly will I hear back after registering?

Usually within a few business days — we'll email to confirm your level, academy, and the next orientation date.

Can I try a session before committing?

Yes — orientation sessions are free, and you're welcome to sit in on a class before registering fully.

What if I'm not sure which academy fits?

Select "Not sure yet" on the form — mentors will help you find a fit during orientation, and you can switch tracks later.

Is there a sibling or family discount?

Ask the team when you register — discounts and scholarships are considered case by case.

Can I pause or cancel my membership?

Yes, membership runs per term with no long-term lock-in — let us know before the next term starts.
