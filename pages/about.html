Our Story

From a small workshop to a growing movement

The Innovative Explorer Club started as a handful of curious students meeting after school to take things apart and put them back together differently. What began with a shared soldering iron and a secondhand robotics kit has grown into a full learning ecosystem spanning ten academies.

Today, TIEC brings together Robotics, Programming, 3D Printing, CAD, Drone Technology, Graphics Design, AI, and Hardware under one roof — mentor-led, hands-on, and built around real problems worth solving.

"Learn. Build. Innovate. Explore."
What drives us

Vision & Mission

Vision

To raise Africa's next generation of innovators, creators, entrepreneurs, engineers, and technology leaders.

Mission

To provide lifelong, hands-on STEM and innovation education that empowers learners to explore, create, invent, collaborate, solve real-world problems, and grow into industry-ready professionals and entrepreneurs through practical learning, mentorship, competitions, and community.

Where we're headed

Long-Term Vision

TIEC today is one chapter of a much bigger plan.

1Now

Deepen One Chapter

Grow every academy in Abuja to full capacity, with a mentor-to-member ratio that keeps learning hands-on.

2Next

Open New Chapters

Bring the same eight-academy model to more cities, each shaped by its local talent and industry.

3Later

A Pan-African Network

Connect chapters across the continent into one exchange, mentorship, and competition network.
